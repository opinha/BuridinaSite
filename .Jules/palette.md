## 2025-05-14 - Accessible Form and Mobile Navigation
**Learning:** Indigenous cultural sites often prioritize visual storytelling, which can lead to gaps in semantic structure and ARIA attributes for interactive elements like custom mobile menus and complex reservation forms.
**Action:** Always check form fields for explicit label-input association using `htmlFor`/`id` and ensure mobile menu toggles have reactive `aria-expanded` and descriptive `aria-label` attributes.
