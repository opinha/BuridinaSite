## 2026-06-16 - [Indigenous Multi-lingual Accessibility]
**Learning:** Accessibility labels must also be localized to maintain the cultural and linguistic integrity of the site, especially when supporting indigenous languages like Inyrybe alongside Portuguese. Skip links and menu controls are often overlooked in translation files but are critical for screen reader users in all supported languages.
**Action:** Always include technical UI labels (skip links, ARIA labels, status messages) in the translation schema and ensure they are updated when new languages are added.
