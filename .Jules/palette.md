
## 2026-07-08 - [Accessibility Foundations: Skip Links and ARIA States]
**Learning:** Indigenous platform navigation requires not just translation of content but also of accessibility helpers. Using 'role="group"' for language selectors and 'aria-current="page"' on links provides a robust semantic structure for assistive technologies.
**Action:** Always include localized 'skip-to-content' links and ensure mobile menus have clear 'aria-expanded'/'aria-controls' associations linked to specific container IDs.
